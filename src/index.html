Questions

What do I need to play?

A Meta Quest headset, a Meta account and an internet connection. {{gameName}} is online multiplayer, so you need to be connected to play with others.

Is there voice chat?

Yes. Players in the same room can hear each other. You can mute anyone you don't want to hear. Voice is streamed live and is not recorded by us.

How do I report someone?

Use the Meta Quest report tool from your headset, or follow the steps on our support page. Include the player's name and what happened.

How do I get my data deleted?

Follow the steps on the data deletion page. We delete your account data within 30 days of confirming the request.

How do I contact the team?

Email {{contactEmail}}. More options are on the support page.
