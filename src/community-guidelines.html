Community guidelines

Everyone on the station deserves to have a good time. These rules apply in every room, to names, voice chat and behaviour.

Be decent to each other

  • No harassment, bullying, stalking or following someone who has asked you to stop.
  • No hate speech or slurs about anyone's race, ethnicity, religion, nationality, gender, sexuality, disability or age.
  • No threats of violence, including as a joke.
  • Trash talk in the arena is fine. Making it personal is not.

Keep it suitable for everyone

  • No sexual content, sexual comments or sexual gestures with your avatar.
  • No graphic or shocking content, including in names.
  • No soundboards, screaming, or playing loud music into voice chat to disrupt others.

Protect younger players

  • Never ask anyone for personal information such as their real name, age, school, address, phone number or photos.
  • Never ask a player to move to another app, server or private chat.
  • Never share your own personal information in the game.
  • Anyone who targets children will be banned permanently and reported to Meta and, where appropriate, the authorities.

Play fair

  • No cheats, mods, hacks, bots, exploits or modified game files.
  • No selling, trading or giving away accounts or items for real money.
  • No pretending to be a member of the {{gameName}} team or a moderator.
  • No spam, scams or advertising.

Names

Your in-game name must follow these same rules. We may reset any name that breaks them.

What happens if the rules are broken

Depending on how serious it is, we may give a warning, mute someone, reset their name, suspend them for a while or ban them permanently. Serious harm, threats or anything targeting children leads straight to a permanent ban. You can appeal a decision from the support page.

Seen something?

Mute the player, then report them. Reports are private, and the player is not told who reported them.
