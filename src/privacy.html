Who we are

{{gameName}} is developed and published by {{developerName}} ("we", "us"). We are the data controller for the personal data described in this policy, which means we decide how and why it is used.

Contact us about privacy at {{contactEmail}}.

What we collect

{{gameName}} is distributed through the Meta Horizon Store. To play, you need a Meta account, which is managed by Meta under Meta's own privacy policy. This policy covers the data that {{gameName}} itself handles.

Information from the Meta platform

  • Your app-scoped Meta user ID and Meta username or display name, used to check that you own the game and to identify your account.
  • Your age group as reported by Meta (for example, whether the account belongs to a preteen, teen or adult), so that the game can apply the right settings. We do not receive your date of birth.
  • Records of in-app purchases you make through Meta, such as which item you bought and when. Meta handles payment, and we never receive your card or bank details.

Your game account (stored with PlayFab)

  • A player ID and the identifier you sign in with (your Meta user ID or a device identifier).
  • The in-game name you choose.
  • Your progress, statistics, cosmetics, inventory and any virtual currency balance.
  • Account status, such as warnings or bans, and the reason for them.
  • Sign-in times, the game version, your headset model and operating system version, and your approximate country, which is worked out from your IP address. We do not collect your precise location.

Multiplayer and voice chat (handled by Photon)

  • Your IP address and a Photon user ID, which are needed to connect you to game servers and other players.
  • Your in-game name, avatar appearance and the room you are in.
  • Your real-time avatar movement, including the position of your headset and controllers, which is sent to other players in your room so they can see you move. It is not stored.
  • Your voice, when you use voice chat. Voice is streamed live through Photon's servers to the players in your room. We do not record or store voice chat.

Reports, support and the contact form

  • If you report a player, or someone reports you, we keep the report, the names and IDs involved, and what action we took.
  • If you message us with the contact form or by email, we receive the reason you chose, what you write, and your email address and in-game name if you give them, so we can reply and help you. We use it only for that.

What we do not collect

  • We do not collect or store passthrough camera images, room scans, eye tracking or face tracking data.
  • We do not collect advertising IDs, show ads, or use your data to target advertising.
  • We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it with data brokers.

Who we share it with

  • Exit Games (Photon) runs our multiplayer and voice servers. It processes your IP address, Photon ID and live game and voice traffic for us under a data processing agreement. See the Photon data processing addendum.
  • Microsoft (Azure PlayFab) stores your game account and progress for us under Microsoft's data protection terms. See the Microsoft privacy statement.
  • Meta provides the platform, ownership checks, purchases and the Meta Quest report tool. Meta's handling of your data is covered by Meta's privacy policy.
  • Other players see and hear your in-game name, avatar, movement and voice while you are in the same room.
  • Web3Forms (web3forms.com), with FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) as a backup, delivers messages sent with our contact form to our inbox. They receive what you type into the form and your IP address in order to do that.
  • Our website host serves this website and keeps short-term technical logs (see This website).
  • Authorities, if the law requires it, or where we need to protect someone from serious harm, including reporting child safety concerns to the right organisations.

Children and teens

Players use {{gameName}} through Meta accounts. Meta decides who can have an account, and children under 13 can only use Meta Quest with an account set up and managed by a parent. Parents can use Meta's parental supervision tools to control which apps their child can use and whether they can use voice chat with others.

For younger players we keep data to the minimum needed to run the game, we do not show ads, and we do not ask for information such as real names, addresses or phone numbers. Players can mute anyone, and our community guidelines forbid asking other players for personal information.

If you are a parent or carer and want to see, correct or delete the data we hold about your child, email {{contactEmail}}. If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child under 13 without the consent required by law, including the United States Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to:

  • get a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • have it corrected if it is wrong
  • have it deleted
  • restrict or object to how we use it
  • receive it in a portable format
  • withdraw consent where we rely on consent

Residents of California and other US states have the right to know, delete and correct their personal information, and the right not to be discriminated against for using these rights. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising.

To use any of these rights, email {{contactEmail}} with your in-game name. We may ask for a detail only the account owner would know to confirm it is you. We reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, please tell us first. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk, or to the data protection authority where you live.

Deleting your data

You can ask us to delete your {{gameName}} data at any time. The steps are on the data deletion page. Uninstalling the game does not delete your account.

Security

Your data is stored with established providers that use encryption in transit and access controls. Only people who need access to run and moderate the game can see account data. No system is perfectly secure, so if we ever have a breach that puts you at risk, we will tell you and the relevant authorities as the law requires.

This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking scripts, and it does not load fonts or code from other companies' servers. It remembers your sound and music settings in your own browser's storage, which never leaves your device. The contact form only sends anything when you press Send. Our hosting provider, Tencent EdgeOne, receives your IP address and basic browser information when you visit, as every website host does, and keeps short-term logs to deliver and protect the site.

Changes and contact

We will update this policy if the game or the law changes. The effective date at the top shows when it last changed. If a change is significant, we will tell players in the game or on this website before it takes effect.
